# Settlement and tax evidence review — 2026-11-27
Primary rule: https://cdsc.com.np/cmsettlementprocedure and https://cdsc.com.np/faq/FAQ.pdf confirm T+2 working-day settlement. A trading calendar alone is not proof of the settlement calendar or completed custody transfer.
Secondary tax evidence:
- https://pkf.trunco.com.np/files/publications/1750835142_pkf-wwtg-2025-2026_online.pdf reports 7.5% up to 365 days and 5% over 365 days.
- https://cma.com.np/knowledge-centre/articles/capital-gains-tax-on-listed-shares-in-nepal-fy-2083-84.html reports Finance Act 2083 baseline 10% and 7.5%, later Gazette 5% and 3.75%.
- https://www.sharesansar.com/newsdetail/capital-gains-tax-rates-reduced-decision-published-in-gazette-2026-09-22 reports implementation September 22.
These are candidate schedules for qualifying resident individuals, NOT approved statutory fixtures. Obtain original acts, Gazette and transition instructions. Do not assume rates for entities or nonresidents. No automatic historical promotion.
