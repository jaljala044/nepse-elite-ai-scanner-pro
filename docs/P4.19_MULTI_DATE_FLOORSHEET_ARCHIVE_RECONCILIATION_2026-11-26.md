# P4.19 — Multi-date pinned floorsheet archive reconciliation (2026-11-26)

## Executive result

The exact pinned SamirWagle and SocrateAI source objects were downloaded and compared for three overlapping dates: 2026-11-19, 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-24.

Across the three dates:
- **133,114 exact shared contract records**
- **76 SocrateAI-only contract records**
- **0 Samir-only contract records**
- **0 shared transaction-field conflicts**
- **0 rejected rows**
- **0 quantity × rate amount exceptions** in the pair audits

Every Samir contract key was present in the corresponding SocrateAI date file. The one-sided SocrateAI rows remain a review ledger; they are not automatically missing ordinary-equity trades.

## Interpretation

1. The three-date comparison shows strong contract-set and field-level consistency between the two pinned repositories for all shared records.
2. SocrateAI contains additional records on each of the three dates. The count and aggregate value of those records vary materially by date.
3. The 2026-11-24 one-sided set was separately classified in P4.18: 16 RSY2 mutual-fund records, one NMLBBLP promoter-share record and one ICFCD89 debenture record. That classification must not be generalized to the 2026-11-19 or 2026-11-20 one-sided sets; their instrument-level ledger still requires classification.
4. The Samir scraper is MeroLagani-derived and writes its capture date into the date field. SocrateAI's upstream capture route is not independently verified. Exact overlap does not establish independent source lineage.
5. No official NEPSE daily contract totals or complete per-security control totals were established for all three dates. The source comparison is not proof of exchange completeness.
6. The observed source date list is not a verified session calendar. No absence from either file may be converted to NO_TRADE.
