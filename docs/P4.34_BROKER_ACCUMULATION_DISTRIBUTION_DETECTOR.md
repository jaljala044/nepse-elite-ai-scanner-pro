# P4.34 — Broker accumulation/distribution candidate detector

This module evaluates a five-session, point-in-time broker party dataset alongside verified stock price/volume and market/sector context.

## Inputs and admission
- Complete broker-session inventory for all five sessions.
- Date-matched, complete floorsheet coverage and verified source/session status.
- Matched buyer and seller party rows for every contract, unique source row lineage, immutable source payload SHA256, and effective identity mapping.
- Minimum broker identity amount coverage (default 80%).
- Verified OHLCV price basis and corporate-action status.
- Verified NEPSE and sector context for every session.
- Timezone-aware decision cutoff; rows arriving after it are excluded.

## Outputs
Descriptive early accumulation/distribution candidates, daily broker breadth, flow acceleration, price/volume context, identity coverage, missing evidence and invalidation conditions. Incomplete sessions or unmatched parties produce INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.

## Interpretation boundary
A candidate is not proof of beneficial ownership, intent, operator activity, manipulation, or future returns. The detector is uncalibrated, research-only, emits no probability or trading action, and is not production eligible. It must be independently backtested and validated before any scanner scoring or BUY/SELL action is enabled.
