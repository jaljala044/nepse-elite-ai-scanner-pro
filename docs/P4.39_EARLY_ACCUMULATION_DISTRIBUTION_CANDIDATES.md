# P4.39 — Early Accumulation & Distribution Candidate Classifier

Combines four independently sourced streams: verified broker net flow/persistence, verified OHLCV volume and structure, stock relative strength versus NEPSE, and sector excess return.

## Admission gates
Every stream must be verified, at least 95% covered, available by the requested point-in-time cutoff, and collectively contain at least three distinct source IDs. Missing, late, unverified or low-coverage evidence withholds the result.

Accumulation requires positive broker net flow with >=60% positive-net session persistence, bullish structure, >=1.2x 20-session average volume with demand absorption, and at least one of positive relative strength, positive sector excess return or supportive price location. Distribution uses symmetric negative-flow, bearish-structure and supply-absorption requirements.

## Output
early_accumulation_candidate, distribution_risk_candidate, no_confirmed_candidate, or withheld. Candidates are WATCH_FOR_CONFIRMATION only; otherwise NO_TRADE. No probability, BUY/SELL, entry, stop or target is generated.

## Interpretation boundary
Broker net flow is descriptive and cannot prove beneficial ownership, intent, operator activity, accumulation or distribution. Further independent confirmation, market regime, liquidity and executable levels are required before trade planning. No backtest performance is claimed.
