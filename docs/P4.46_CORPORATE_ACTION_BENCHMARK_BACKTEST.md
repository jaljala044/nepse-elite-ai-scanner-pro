# P4.46 — Corporate-Action-Aware Price History & Benchmark Comparison

## API
POST /v1/research/backtest/corporate-action-benchmark

This research endpoint accepts a symbol's OHLCV rows, caller-attested corporate-action records, a portfolio equity curve, and benchmark OHLCV. It returns a backward-adjusted research OHLCV series and an exact-date portfolio/benchmark return comparison.

## Corporate-action handling
- Bonus and split ratios are additional shares per existing share. Pre-ex-date OHLC prices are multiplied by 1/(1+ratio); historical volume is divided by that factor.
- Cash dividends adjust pre-ex-date prices by (prior close - cash per share)/prior close. Dividend does not alter share volume.
- Rights issues use theoretical ex-rights price: (prior close + rights ratio × subscription price)/(1 + rights ratio). The implied factor adjusts pre-ex-date prices; rights terms are not treated as verified subscription/fill events.
- Only actions with status=verified, source_id, timezone-aware available_at no later than evidence_cutoff, and ex_date no later than as_of_date are admitted.
- Future ex-dates are omitted. Future-known actions are rejected as look-ahead.
- Each applied action is retained in an audit list with source ID and factor.

## Benchmark comparison
- Portfolio equity and benchmark close are matched on exact common dates only.
- No forward-fill, interpolation, or synthetic observations.
- Returns are measured from first to last common date; excess return is portfolio return minus benchmark return.
- Output is descriptive only; it does not establish alpha, predictive edge, or strategy validity.

## Important limitations
- Corporate-action status and source attestations are caller supplied and are not independently certified against NEPSE/CDSC.
- Backward-adjusted OHLCV is a research history transform, not an executable price series or realized total-return ledger.
- The endpoint does not rewrite portfolio holdings, create bonus shares, debit rights subscription cash, or credit dividend cash. The submitted portfolio equity curve must already contain those modeled share/cash effects before benchmark comparison.
- Rights issue adjustment is theoretical and assumes the supplied ratio and subscription price are complete and correct.
- No external benchmark data is fetched; benchmark symbol and rows are caller supplied.
- production_eligible=false; no order routing or investment recommendation.

## Verification
Unit tests cover bonus, split, cash dividend and rights adjustments; source/timezone/look-ahead checks; duplicate OHLCV dates; exact-date benchmark alignment; and API fail-closed behavior.
