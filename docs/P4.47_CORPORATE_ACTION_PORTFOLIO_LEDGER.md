# P4.47 — Corporate-Action Portfolio Holdings & Cash Ledger

## API
POST /v1/research/portfolio/corporate-actions/apply

This endpoint replays caller-supplied corporate-action events against an explicit holdings snapshot and cash balance. It returns updated quantities, average costs, cost basis, cash, and an immutable-style event audit in the response.

## Supported events
- Bonus issues: additional shares are credited on the supplied listing date; aggregate cost basis is preserved and average cost is recalculated.
- Splits: additional shares are credited using the supplied additional-shares-per-existing-share ratio; aggregate cost basis is preserved.
- Cash dividends: entitlement quantity × cash per share is credited on the supplied payment date.
- Rights issues: only explicit subscribed_quantity is modeled. Subscription cash is debited on subscription_payment_date; subscribed shares and their subscription cost are added on listing_date. Unsubscribed entitlements are not assumed to be subscribed.

## Evidence and controls
- Every action requires verified status, a non-empty source ID, timezone-aware available_at no later than evidence_cutoff, unique action_id and an action-specific effective date.
- Entitlement quantity is explicit and checked against replayed holdings at each event date.
- Rights subscribed quantity cannot exceed entitlement quantity.
- Insufficient cash for rights subscription fails closed.
- Events after as_of_date are not applied.
- All outputs are research-only and production_eligible=false.

## Critical integration boundary
This module does not infer record-date ownership. The supplied holdings must be a starting snapshot, and each entitlement_quantity must be reconciled against CDSC/custodian records for the relevant record date. The endpoint does not ingest portfolio trades or replay buy/sell activity between the snapshot and corporate-action dates. It therefore cannot independently establish entitlement eligibility.

The ledger is not yet automatically wired into P4.45's daily portfolio simulation. A future integration should replay transactions and corporate actions in one dated event stream, using verified ex-dates, record dates, payment dates, listing dates and custody reconciliations before calculating total-return equity.

## Limitations
- Caller-supplied corporate-action data is not independently verified against NEPSE/CDSC.
- No withholding tax, fractional-share cash-in-lieu, rights renunciation, oversubscription, failed allotment, financing cost or cash interest.
- Bonus/split cost basis preserves total basis; tax-lot-specific accounting is not implemented.
- Rights subscription assumes the supplied subscribed quantity is actually allotted and listed.
- No brokerage/custodian statement reconciliation or order execution.
