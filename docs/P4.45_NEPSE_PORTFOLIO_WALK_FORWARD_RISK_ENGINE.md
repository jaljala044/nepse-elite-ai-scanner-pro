# P4.45 — NEPSE Portfolio-Level Walk-Forward & Risk Simulation

## API
`POST /v1/research/backtest/portfolio`

The endpoint consumes stored immutable P4.42 research signals, P4.44 execution scenarios, reviewed daily OHLCV, caller-attested verified calendars, and explicit cost/execution assumptions.

## Portfolio controls
- Initial cash capital.
- Maximum concurrent positions.
- Maximum single-position allocation as a fraction of prior-marked equity.
- Maximum sector allocation as a fraction of prior-marked equity.
- Risk budget per trade, sized against entry-to-stop price distance.
- Cash availability and whole-share sizing.
- Duplicate frozen signals are removed by canonical content hash.
- Duplicate same-symbol concurrent positions are withheld.
- Missing sector mapping, calendar, OHLCV, cost assumptions, or execution inputs fail closed.

## Chronological accounting
- Candidate signals are ordered by scenario entry session, decision timestamp and content hash.
- Exits are booked before entries on the same session.
- New positions are sized using cash plus prior-session close marks only; current-session close is not used to size that day's entries.
- Entry cash uses the fee-inclusive P4.44 buy cash scenario.
- Exit proceeds use fee- and tax-inclusive P4.44 sell cash scenario.
- Open positions are marked to each verified daily close. A missing mark bar is a hard error.
- Equity curve includes date, cash, market value, equity and open-position count.
- Drawdown is calculated from the portfolio equity curve. Sharpe/Sortino are emitted only with at least 30 daily return observations, using zero risk-free rate and 252-session annualization.
- No unfilled/withheld signal is silently recorded as a loss.

## Output classification
Every result is `portfolio_level_hypothetical_execution_scenario_not_exchange_fills`. It is a portfolio accounting simulation using P4.44 hypothetical execution scenarios, not actual NEPSE fills, realized P&L or an investment recommendation.

## Important limits
- Cost rates, taxes, price bands, tick size, turnover threshold and participation are caller-supplied assumptions; not certified historical official schedules.
- Sector mapping is caller supplied and must be audited independently.
- Daily OHLCV cannot prove intraday fills, order-book queue priority, auction matching, market impact or partial executions.
- No dividend, bonus, rights, split, financing cost, cash interest or tax-lot treatment is modeled.
- The stop-distance risk budget is a sizing control, not a guaranteed maximum loss; gaps and circuit locks can exceed it.
- The portfolio calendar and per-symbol calendars are caller-attested; per-symbol calendars must include two following sessions after each exit to establish when sale proceeds become spendable. The endpoint does not independently query NEPSE/CDSC.
- No benchmark series or excess-return metric is supplied.
- The endpoint is a chronological portfolio accounting simulation over supplied frozen signals; it does not itself create train/validation/test partitions, optimize parameters, or establish strategy edge. P4.43 remains the separate signal-level walk-forward validation stage.
