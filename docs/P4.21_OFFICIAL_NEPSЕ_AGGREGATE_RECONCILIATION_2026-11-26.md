## 4. Interpretation and limits

1. The NEPSE endpoint is a primary-source daily aggregate control, and the exact raw response is now preserved and hash-verifiable.
2. SocrateAI is numerically consistent with the official aggregate totals for all three pinned dates. This is strong aggregate corroboration, not proof that every individual contract in the archive is correct.
3. Exact aggregate agreement does not establish SocrateAI's upstream data lineage or prove it is independent of other publishers. Its source independence remains unverified.
4. Samir's daily file is a Merolagani-derived scraper capture; its date field is generated by the scraper and is not itself an exchange-issued trade-date field. The aggregate deficits are real comparisons against the observed file contents, but must not be labelled missing ordinary-equity trades without scope and provenance.
5. The market-summary-history endpoint is aggregate-only. It does not provide full per-security totals, individual contracts, broker attribution, floorsheet completeness or order-book evidence.
6. The summary record's presence or absence does not by itself prove whether a date was an official open session, holiday, or zero-trade session.
7. The 20-Nov third-party Nepse Decode conflicting summary remains an unresolved secondary-source discrepancy; the captured official NEPSE row agrees with SocrateAI's aggregate values, but the conflicting publisher's raw payload/provenance still has not been examined.
