## Disposition

The public controls support SocrateAI's aggregate totals numerically on the three sampled dates, but they do not establish that SocrateAI is independent of the other publishers or that the archive is complete. Samir's one-sided differences are fully explained by currently out-of-scope instrument classes in P4.20, subject to the historical identity caveat.

No source is designated canonical. Do not admit TRADED/NO_TRADE coverage cells, broker footprints, training records, backtests or BUY signals from this comparison. Preserve Nepse Decode's conflict as a separate exception rather than silently discarding it.
