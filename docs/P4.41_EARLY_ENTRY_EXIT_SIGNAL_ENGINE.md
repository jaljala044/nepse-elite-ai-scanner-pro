## Contract
The endpoint accepts a point-in-time snapshot containing:
- `market`: verified regime, coverage, source IDs, availability timestamp.
- `sector`: verified alignment and 1M excess return.
- `liquidity`: verified median turnover, spread, trade count and displayed depth.
- `broker`: verified net amount and positive-net session persistence.
- `price_volume`: verified OHLCV/structure evidence.
- `candidate`: verified P4.39 candidate classification.
- `plans`: separate `t_plus_2`, `swing`, and `positional` plans.

Each evidence stream must be marked verified, meet the 95% default coverage threshold, have source IDs, and be available by the requested cutoff. The caller is responsible for upstream verification; this endpoint does not re-open or independently attest source files.

## Signal gates
- Market regime must be bull, accumulation, or markup.
- Sector must be leading/improving with positive excess return.
- Liquidity defaults: median turnover >= NPR 1,000,000; median spread <= 2%; median trade count >= 20; median displayed depth >= NPR 100,000. These are configurable policy defaults, not official NEPSE exchange thresholds.
- Broker net flow must be positive and positive-net session persistence >= 60%; this is corroboration only and never a standalone trigger or proof of ownership/intent.
- Distribution-risk candidate blocks new long signals.
- Each horizon requires a confirmed setup, supported entry type, structural stop, dated inferred chart-level provenance, invalidation condition, and nearest-target gross reward/risk >= 2:1.
- Missing/late/unverified/low-coverage common evidence returns `NO_TRADE` for all horizons. Missing or invalid horizon geometry withholds only that horizon.

## Output and limitations
The engine returns separate T+2, swing and positional actions, entry, stop, T1-T3, risk percentage, gross reward/risk, invalidation, evidence dates and reason codes. Probabilities are always null. It does not currently issue SELL/exit orders, simulate fills, deduct transaction costs/slippage, validate circuit-limit execution, or claim a backtested edge. `BUY` means a research signal passed the supplied evidence contract, not an instruction or order.

No investment-horizon signal is emitted in this phase. No live NEPSE/floorsheet connector is added. A later integration must supply independently verified, point-in-time artifacts and execution-cost/settlement controls before production use.
