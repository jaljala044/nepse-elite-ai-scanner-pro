# P4.38 — Broker profile scanner context bridge

## Scope
Connects P4.37 rolling broker/security profiles to the feature snapshot as a separate descriptive context object. It does not change the weighted V3 scoring contract.

## Admission
- Requires the exact P4.37 complete-source/no-zero-fill policy identifier.
- Requires timezone-aware profile and decision timestamps; profiles after the scanner decision are withheld.
- Requires descriptive-only rows, unique broker/security/window identities, full requested windows and at least 95% complete-session coverage.
- Requires a minimum of three distinct brokers per security/window before directional breadth is labelled.
- Reports counts of net-buy-dominant, net-sell-dominant and balanced brokers. It deliberately does not sum broker net amounts because buyer and seller parties are opposite sides of the same matched contracts.

## Output boundary
The feature snapshot exposes `broker_behavior_profiles` separately from `component_feature_bundles`. Scores remain null and `score_eligible=false`; no probabilities or BUY/SELL/HOLD decisions are emitted. This is context for future cross-validation with price/volume, market/sector conditions and the separate broker accumulation/distribution detector.

## Limitations
The bridge cannot prove beneficial ownership, operator intent, circular trading, accumulation or distribution. P4.37 profiles remain dependent on independently verified source completeness and identity mapping. Synthetic tests validate contract behaviour, not live NEPSE source coverage or trading performance.
