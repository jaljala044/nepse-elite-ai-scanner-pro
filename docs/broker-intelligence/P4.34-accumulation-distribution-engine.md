# P4.34 — Broker Accumulation and Distribution Candidate Engine

Status: implementation specification; research-only; not production eligible.

## Purpose

Convert admitted, point-in-time broker execution-channel observations into explainable **candidate patterns** by combining repeated flow with price/volume response and independently verified NEPSE/sector context. This component must not infer beneficial ownership, client identity, intent, or manipulation.

## Dependency and admission contract

P4.34 is stacked on P4.33. It may consume only records admitted by the P4.33 batch gate and retrievable from the P4.32 append-only memory. It must also receive a point-in-time market-context bundle whose source, session, finality, availability timestamp, payload digest, and coverage are independently validated.

Reject or return `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` when:
- broker/session identity is unverified, source rights are not cleared, or any input is quarantined;
- the required session inventory is missing, partial, unknown, or below the configured coverage threshold;
- observations are duplicated, conflicting, future-dated, or unavailable at the decision timestamp;
- symbol identity, corporate-action basis, price/volume series, or NEPSE/sector context cannot be reconciled;
- a required feature is absent. Missing values are never treated as zero or neutral.

## Output contract

Every result is descriptive and contains:
- `symbol`, `decision_at`, `as_of_session`, `methodology_version`;
- `status`: `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `NO_CANDIDATE`, or `CANDIDATE`;
- `pattern`: `EARLY_ACCUMULATION_CANDIDATE`, `CONFIRMED_ACCUMULATION_PATTERN`, `EARLY_DISTRIBUTION_CANDIDATE`, or `CONFIRMED_DISTRIBUTION_PATTERN`;
- feature values, denominators, coverage, missing-feature list, source/evidence IDs and payload hashes;
- rule-level explanations, contradictory evidence, invalidation conditions, and point-in-time lineage;
- `calibration_status=UNCALIBRATED` until walk-forward evaluation is complete;
- `trading_action=NONE` and `production_eligible=false`.

Do not emit probability, confidence percentage, BUY/SELL/HOLD, price targets, or manipulation labels in this phase.

## Feature families

### Broker flow persistence
For each execution channel and symbol, calculate buy value, sell value, net value, gross value, net/gross ratio, active-session count, participation share, and consecutive-session persistence over 1D, 1W, 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, and 2Y windows where complete session inventory exists. Keep buyer and seller sides distinct. Preserve broker-code semantics as execution channels only.

Normalize flow against the channel's own trailing baseline and the symbol's turnover. Require minimum observations and report the exact denominator. Do not rank a broker by raw net value alone.

### Price and volume response
Measure close-to-close and intraday price response, range compression/expansion, turnover and volume relative to trailing baselines, close location within range, gap behaviour, and effort-versus-result. Use only bars available at the decision time and adjusted price bases backed by approved corporate-action evidence.

### Market and sector context
Compare symbol return and turnover participation with NEPSE and its verified sector index over matching windows. Require aligned sessions and complete context. A broad-market rally or sector-wide flow must be distinguished from symbol-specific relative strength.

### Structure context
Accept only separately validated support/resistance, swing highs/lows, BOS/CHoCH and supply/demand features. P4.34 does not infer order blocks or liquidity sweeps from missing or low-quality OHLCV.

## Candidate rule design

Rules are conjunctive evidence gates, not a weighted score that can conceal missing inputs.

### Early accumulation candidate
Require all of:
1. Complete, verified flow windows and minimum session count.
2. Repeated positive net flow over multiple non-overlapping sessions, with persistence above a versioned threshold.
3. Flow acceleration relative to the same channel's trailing baseline and materiality relative to symbol turnover.
4. Price not materially advancing despite demand, or a supported reclaim/hold of a validated demand area.
5. Volume/turnover response consistent with absorption rather than a single isolated print.
6. No unresolved high-severity contradiction such as broad distribution, failed structure, or market/sector deterioration.

### Confirmed accumulation pattern
Requires the early candidate plus subsequent independent price/volume confirmation (for example, a validated structure break and successful retest) on later sessions. Confirmation must be timestamped after the candidate; it cannot be backfilled into the original decision.

### Early distribution candidate
Require repeated negative net flow or persistent selling participation, material relative to turnover, together with weak price progress, supply response, or deteriorating relative strength. Selling on a single down day is insufficient.

### Confirmed distribution pattern
Requires the early candidate plus later independent downside structure/price confirmation. It remains descriptive and does not authorize an exit by itself.

## Contradiction and suppression rules

- Strong sector-wide buying without symbol-specific excess flow is classified as sector-led, not broker-specific accumulation.
- Large one-session flow with no persistence is an isolated-flow observation, not an accumulation/distribution pattern.
- High gross turnover with small net flow is not directional accumulation.
- Incomplete source inventory produces UNKNOWN, never zero flow.
- A broker-code change or mapping ambiguity breaks continuity unless independently reconciled.
- Corporate-action or security-identity uncertainty suppresses pattern classification.
- Conflicting buyer/seller evidence is surfaced, not netted away.

## Calibration and validation plan

Before any probability or trade integration:
1. Freeze methodology versions and point-in-time input manifests.
2. Build an event dataset with strict decision-time snapshots and no future leakage.
3. Define outcomes separately for T+2 (3–5 trading sessions), swing (1–4 weeks), and positional (1–6 months); do not pool horizons.
4. Use walk-forward splits by time, purge overlapping outcome windows, and keep all symbols from a date in the same fold where appropriate.
5. Include delisted/suspended securities and failed candidates to avoid survivorship bias.
6. Model NEPSE price limits, liquidity, turnover, spread, circuit locks, slippage, brokerage, taxes, and execution feasibility.
7. Report sample size, coverage, precision/recall, false-positive rate, event return distribution, drawdown, and confidence intervals. Do not report a probability until calibrated on an adequate out-of-sample sample.
8. Compare against simple baselines (price/volume only, flow only, and market/sector context only) to prove incremental value.

## Required tests

- Exact replay is deterministic and idempotent.
- Future event and late-available evidence are excluded at historical decision times.
- Partial/unknown inventory yields `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
- Duplicate and conflicting observations cannot inflate persistence.
- Buyer and seller observations remain separate.
- A single high-volume day cannot confirm a pattern.
- Sector-wide moves do not become symbol-specific broker signals.
- Corporate-action basis mismatch suppresses the candidate.
- Confirmation occurring later cannot alter an earlier snapshot.
- Every output carries methodology version and full source lineage.
- No output path can emit a trading action or production eligibility.

## Exit criteria

P4.34 is complete only when the detector, schemas, tests, documentation, API contract, and point-in-time replay tests are merged; CI passes on the exact PR head; and a reviewer verifies that all outputs remain descriptive-only. Walk-forward calibration is a separate P4.37 gate.
