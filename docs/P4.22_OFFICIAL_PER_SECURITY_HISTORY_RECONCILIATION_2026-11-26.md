## 1. Official historical endpoint confirmed

Two official NEPSE API routes were captured:

1. Bulk daily price-volume: `/api/nots/nepse-data/today-price?businessDate=YYYY-MM-DD` (POST through the pinned NEPSE client).
2. Per-security price-volume history: `/api/nots/market/history/security/{security_id}?size=500&startDate=...&endDate=...` (GET).

Both requests used HTTPS to `nepalstock.com.np`; raw response bytes and SHA-256 digests were archived. The historical per-security endpoint accepted the requested 19–24 November range and returned one page per current equity candidate. The bulk endpoint accepted all three historical business dates.

## 6. Interpretation and limits

- This is strong primary-source per-security corroboration for the 281 current equity candidate symbols and three dates.
- It establishes exact aggregate agreement between positive per-security official daily statistics and both pinned floorsheet sources for those 281 candidates.
- Official zero-activity rows for NLO and BNL are explicit exchange statistics, but they do not by themselves satisfy the project's strict NO_TRADE gate.
- The endpoint's 281-symbol history query is a current-security-ID lookup. The crosswalk IDs match the 2026-11-25 snapshot, but historical effective-dated security lifecycle identity has not been proven.
- The 281-equity universe does not include all instruments traded on NEPSE. The separate bulk endpoint and instrument-scope audit cover the wider traded-symbol set.
- Exact official data agreement does not establish the upstream provenance of Samir or SocrateAI, nor source redistribution rights.
- The capture is limited to 19, 20, 23 and 24 November as returned in the requested 19–24 range. It is not a full session calendar or a complete historical archive.
