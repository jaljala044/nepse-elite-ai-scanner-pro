# P4.43 — NEPSE Historical Backtesting & Walk-Forward Validation

## Scope
P4.43 adds a chronological research backtest over frozen P4.42 signal records and reviewed daily OHLCV. It is long-only and research-only. It does not place orders or claim exchange fills.

## Method
- Deduplicate immutable signal records by canonical SHA-256.
- Sort by decision date and split chronologically into train, validation and test partitions.
- Purge earlier-partition signals whose full verified-session horizon overlaps the next partition boundary.
- Reuse the P4.42 point-in-time OHLCV path evaluator. Missing/unreviewed bars, incomplete horizons, invalid calendars and unfilled entry references are withheld.
- Same-session stop/target ambiguity remains stop-first; stop gaps use the worse opening reference.
- Publish gross price-reference metrics separately for each partition and test as out-of-sample descriptive results.
- Hash the configuration for reproducibility.

## Metrics
Sample size, win rate, average gain/loss, profit factor, expectancy, max drawdown, T1/T2/T3 touch rates and stop rate. Metrics are withheld when there is no eligible sample.

## Explicitly unavailable
Net return, fees/tax adjusted return, slippage, market impact, benchmark excess return, Sharpe/Sortino, actual fill rate, queue priority and partial fills. Existing execution-cost helpers are hypothetical scenarios, not a verified historical NEPSE fee schedule.

## Data requirements
Signals must be frozen research-only records with decision_date, symbol, supported horizon and BUY action. Per-symbol rows must pass the existing reviewed OHLCV eligibility checks and be complete for every verified session in the signal horizon. The verified session calendar must be unique and include the decision session. Caller-supplied calendar/data are not independently fetched by this endpoint.

## Safety and limitations
- No random shuffling; chronological splits only.
- Horizon overlap is purged at partition boundaries.
- SELL/HOLD/NO TRADE records are not interpreted as short positions.
- Daily OHLCV touch is not a fill. This model cannot resolve intraday path ordering beyond conservative stop-first handling.
- Incomplete and unfilled paths are not silently scored as losses.
- Small samples are LOW_CONFIDENCE; no probabilities or strategy-edge claims are emitted.
