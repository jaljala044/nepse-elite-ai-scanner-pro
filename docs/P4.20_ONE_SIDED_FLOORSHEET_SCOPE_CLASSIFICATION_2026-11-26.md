## Interpretation and limitations

1. The 76 source-only records do not represent missing ordinary-equity contracts under the dated snapshot classification. All are classified as mutual funds, promoter shares or debentures.
2. The registry is a **current 2026-11-25 snapshot used as a scope hint**, not a historical effective-dated instrument master for 19 or 20 November. The report explicitly sets `historical_security_identity_verified=false` for every contract. Historical identity/lifecycle verification remains outstanding.
3. This resolves the current-snapshot instrument-scope question only. It does not prove that Samir or SocrateAI daily archives are complete, or that the two sources have independent acquisition lineages.
4. The 20-Nov one-sided set is materially larger than the other dates (39,950 shares / NPR 4,814,040.21). Preserve its full contract ledger and investigate its source acquisition route and date-level controls separately.
5. Do not use these out-of-scope records in ordinary-equity price, liquidity, broker-footprint or scanner scoring features.
6. No one-sided record is a basis for claiming broker manipulation, beneficial ownership, circular trading or operator activity.
