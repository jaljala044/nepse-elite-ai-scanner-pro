# P4.38 — Broker Sector Rotation Intelligence

Compares each broker's turnover mix across the immediately preceding and latest verified exchange-session windows (default 5 vs 5 sessions).

## Evidence rules
- Requires an independently verified NEPSE session calendar and P4.36 complete-source manifests.
- Uses a fixed contiguous calendar window; incomplete dates reduce coverage rather than being skipped to backfill.
- Requires at least 95% complete-source coverage in each comparison window.
- Uses only point-in-time available, descriptive-only observations with explicit security-to-sector mappings.
- Unmapped securities and late/unverified rows are excluded and counted; duplicate natural keys fail closed.

## Outputs
Per broker and sector: buy/sell/net/gross amount in both windows, sector share of broker-side gross activity, share change, and increased/decreased/no-material-change labels. The default materiality threshold is a 5 percentage-point change in sector mix.

## Interpretation
This is a change in the mix of observed broker-side transactions, not a claim about capital ownership, broker intent, institutional accumulation, distribution or future returns. It is not a trade signal. It must be cross-checked against market/sector returns, liquidity, price-volume structure and independent sources.
