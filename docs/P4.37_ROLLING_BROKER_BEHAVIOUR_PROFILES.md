# P4.37 — Rolling Broker Behaviour Profiles

## Step 2 of the P4.36/P4.37 sequence
Calculates broker/security daily and rolling behaviour over 5, 21 and 252 verified NEPSE exchange sessions.

## Inputs
- P4.36 daily observation ledger.
- Independently verified NEPSE exchange-session calendar.
- P4.36 complete-source session manifests. A session counts only if source coverage, source controls, broker identity coverage and exchange-session verification all passed.
- Point-in-time event-date and availability timestamp cutoffs.

## Metrics
For each broker/security/window:
- buy, sell, gross and net amount;
- buy, sell and net quantity;
- active, positive-net, negative-net and zero-activity sessions;
- positive/negative net session persistence ratios;
- gross broker participation versus all verified broker-side activity for that security;
- per-session daily totals and participation.

## Missing data and coverage
Only complete-source sessions can contribute. An absent broker/security record on such a session is a valid zero-activity day because the entire source extract, source controls and identity coverage were certified complete. An absent or incomplete session is not zero-filled and reduces the window coverage ratio. A profile is marked insufficient_evidence and its direction is withheld when coverage is below 95% or fewer than the full requested exchange sessions exist.

## Interpretation boundary
Labels are net_buy_dominant, net_sell_dominant, balanced_or_no_activity or insufficient_evidence. These are transaction-flow descriptions only, not proof of beneficial ownership, accumulation, distribution, operator intent or BUY/SELL signals. Additional independent evidence and price/volume/sector context are required before any scanner action.
