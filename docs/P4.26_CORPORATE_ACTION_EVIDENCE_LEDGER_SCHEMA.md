# P4.26 — Corporate-action evidence ledger schema

**Status:** schema and empty intake template only; no corporate-action records admitted.

## Why these fields

CDSC's corporate-action documentation identifies bonus, rights, dividend, merger and split as distinct corporate-action types. Its equity disclosure form requests the action/allotment type, ISIN, record date, last trade date, ratio, CA sequence number, and allotment/distribution date. The ledger therefore retains these source facts separately rather than collapsing them into one effective date.

Primary references:
- CDSC, Documentation for Corporate Action: https://cdsc.com.np/documentation
- CDSC, Disclosure of Book Closure Information (equity form): https://cdsc.com.np/downloads_files/2021_01_03_04_34_13_book_close_discloser_information_form.pdf

## Admission rules

1. Preserve the original source document bytes under an immutable evidence path and record its SHA-256.
2. `announced_at` and `available_at` must be timezone-aware timestamps. `available_at` is the earliest evidenced public-availability time, not a guessed date.
3. Keep announcement, last-trade, record, ex, and allotment/distribution dates distinct. Do not infer one from another.
4. Store ISIN and NEPSE security ID separately. A current-snapshot ID alone is not historical identity proof; leave `security_id` null and `identity_status=unresolved` until supported by dated primary NEPSE/CDSC evidence.
5. `factor` is optional and must remain null unless the exact adjustment factor and its method are evidenced. Preserve the issuer's ratio/terms independently.
6. Secondary sources may be retained as leads/context, but do not satisfy the existing primary-source adjustment gate.
7. `review_status=independently_reviewed_confirmed` requires reviewer identity and review timestamp. This ledger does not itself apply an adjustment.
8. Never modify raw OHLCV. Any later adjustment must remain a separate, point-in-time, independently reviewed derived dataset.
