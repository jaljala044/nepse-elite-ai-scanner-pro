# P4.44 — NEPSE Realistic Execution & Transaction-Cost Backtesting

## Scope
P4.44 adds a conservative execution-scenario layer on top of P4.42 frozen signals and reviewed OHLCV paths. It reuses the existing P4.43 research signal path and the explicit P4.40-era cost/constraint helpers. It does not submit orders or claim fills.

## API
`POST /v1/research/backtest/execution-scenario`

Required inputs:
- Frozen BUY `signal_id`
- `evaluation_date`, positive whole-share `quantity`
- Caller-attested verified sessions and point-in-time calendar evidence (status verified, source ID, >=95% coverage, timezone-aware availability no later than evaluation cutoff)
- Explicit `cost_assumptions`: buy/sell brokerage rates, regulatory rate, sell DP charge, capital-gains-tax scenario rate, slippage bps, minimum brokerage fee
- Explicit `execution_config`: tick size, maximum participation fraction, minimum turnover proxy and dated price-band lower/upper references

## Execution scenario rules
- Only complete reviewed OHLCV paths are considered.
- Entry reference is the first daily bar that touches the planned entry. Buy price is adjusted for supplied slippage and rounded adversely upward to the supplied tick.
- Exit reference is the stop reference (including conservative gap treatment) or the end-of-horizon mark. Sell price is adjusted for supplied slippage and rounded adversely downward.
- Quantity is capped by the minimum of requested quantity and participation capacity on both entry and exit sessions. This is a partial-quantity scenario, not evidence of partial fills.
- Daily turnover proxy is close × volume and must clear the caller-supplied minimum.
- Supplied dated price bands are required; bars outside the band are withheld. A daily bar locked at upper limit on entry or lower limit on exit is not assumed executable.
- Exit is withheld unless two subsequent verified sessions establish settlement eligibility.
- Brokerage, regulatory charge, DP charge and simplified positive-gain tax are calculated with the explicit caller-supplied scenario rates. Slippage is applied to prices once, before fees.

## Evidence and confidence
Reviewed OHLCV is gated through the existing eligibility assessor. Calendar evidence is caller-attested and not independently fetched. Price-band references, tick size, turnover threshold, participation limit and all cost rates are scenario inputs; this implementation does not certify them as historical official schedules. Every response is classified `hypothetical_execution_scenario_not_fill` and `production_eligible=false`.

## Limitations
- No order-book depth, queue priority, intraday path, auction matching, partial fill evidence or actual broker contract notes.
- Turnover proxy is not verified exchange turnover.
- Circuit lock detection is conservative but cannot infer executable opposing liquidity from daily OHLCV.
- Settlement eligibility is based on supplied verified session dates, not an independent CDSC settlement record.
- Fee and tax assumptions are not official schedule attestations or tax advice.
- No automated bulk walk-forward net-performance claim is emitted by this endpoint. A future release should run these scenarios across every out-of-sample trade and summarize coverage/withheld reasons before reporting net metrics.
- No live execution, brokerage integration or investment recommendation is made.
